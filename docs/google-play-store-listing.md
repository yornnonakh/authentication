# Finsight store listing draft

## App name

Finsight: Expense Tracker

## Short description

Track expenses, plan monthly budgets, and understand your spending.

## Full description

Get a clearer picture of your money with Finsight.

Record income and expenses, organize transactions by category, and see how your
spending changes from month to month. Set a monthly budget and check your progress
as you go.

- Add and edit income and expense records with dates, categories, and notes.
- See your balance, monthly totals, and spending breakdowns.
- Compare income and expenses with the previous month.
- Search your history and filter transactions by type and category.
- Set a monthly spending goal and monitor budget progress.
- Export your transactions to CSV through your device's share sheet.
- Choose a light theme, dark theme, or your device's default appearance.

Sign in with email or Google and personalize your profile. Transactions and
budgets are stored per account on the device where you enter them. Export a copy
for backup; finance records do not sync across devices. Amounts display in USD.
